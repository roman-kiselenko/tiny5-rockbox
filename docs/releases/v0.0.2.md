# H2 Rockbox v0.0.2 — progressive JPEGs, and a release pipeline

Follows [v0.0.1](v0.0.1.md). Two functional changes and the way releases are
made.

**Archive:** `rockbox-h2-v0.0.2.zip` — unzip to the root of the SD card (it
contains `.rockbox/`).

## Album covers: progressive JPEGs

`apps/recorder/jpeg_load.c` now decodes **progressive** JPEGs as well as
baseline ones, scan by scan into a 2 MiB static coefficient buffer
(`JPEG_PROG_BUF_SIZE`), keeping only the coefficients the downscaling IDCT
needs and the per-block nonzero mask the refinement scans need, in stripes of
MCU rows — at most `JPEG_PROG_MAX_PASSES` (4) passes over the file. A cover too
big for that buffer fails with -13 and shows the placeholder instead of
corrupting the row. Covers are still decoded once and cached pre-scaled in
`.rockbox/newui/thumbs`.

## Now Playing

The track information block is centred rather than hung on the left edge, so the
title, artist, album and the codec · bitrate · sample rate line read as one
unit above the progress band.

## The fonts on the card

The Noto Sans CJK faces are no longer committed: `fonts/newui/newui-14.fnt`,
`newui-18.fnt`, `newui-20bold.fnt`, `newui-22bold.fnt` and `newui-40bold.fnt`
are gone from the tree (27 MB) and `tools/buildzip.pl` no longer packs them.
Tiny5 Mono is the only installed font; `fonts/newui/mkfonts.sh` plus
`tools/convttf` are kept as the seed for a future CJK build. Delete
`.rockbox/fonts/newui-*.fnt` on the card to reclaim the space.

## Releases

Pushing a `vX.Y.Z` tag runs `.github/workflows/release.yml`: it builds
`--type=n`, renames `rockbox.zip` to `rockbox-vX.Y.Z.zip` (the archive was called
`rockbox-h2-vX.Y.Z.zip` until this release) and publishes it on
the tag's release. `.github/workflows/ci.yml` builds firmware, simulator and
warble on every push to `master`, and runs the simulator smoke test
(`ci/smoke.script`).

## Release notes per tag

From v0.0.3 onward each tag carries its own notes file in `docs/releases/`.
